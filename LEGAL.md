security, or a substitute for advice from a qualified, licensed financial

ARIA is built to stay educational (see [docs/ADVICE_POLICY.md](docs/ADVICE_POLICY.md)):
it won't tell you to buy, sell or hold a specific security, shows model
portfolios by risk band rather than personal picks, never promises returns,
and declines requests involving insider information or market manipulation.

## 2. AI-generated content may be wrong

ARIA's responses are generated by large language models, which can
misstate facts, misinterpret data, or "hallucinate" plausible-sounding but
incorrect information — even when a number carries a citation marker
(`[^pe]`, `[^roce]`, etc.) or a stated confidence level. Verify anything
material against the underlying source before acting on it.

## 3. Data accuracy

Market data, fundamentals, news, and mutual fund NAVs come from third-party

that ARIA does not control. These sources can be delayed, incomplete, or
wrong. No guarantee of accuracy, completeness, or timeliness is made for
any data point, chart, or figure ARIA presents.

## 5. Bring-your-own-key: where your data actually goes

If you configure your own LLM API key (Settings tab), your prompts —
including anything you type in chat, plus data ARIA assembles into a
prompt on your behalf (stock data, your financial profile, etc.) — are
sent directly to **the provider and model you configured**, not just to
this instance's operator. That provider's own terms and privacy policy
govern how they handle that data; the ARIA operator has no visibility into
or control over it. If you use the free trial instead, the same applies to
whichever of the operator's own configured providers (e.g. Groq, Google
Gemini) handles that request.

## 6. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account and use ARIA.

## 7. What's collected, and why

- **Account data**: email address, a salted/hashed password (never stored
  in plaintext), and a session token — needed to log you in and keep your
  data scoped to your account.
- **Chat history, reports, screeners, alerts, watchlist, financial
  profile**: stored so your conversations, saved work, and alert
  conditions persist across sessions. This is the actual product — ARIA
  can't remember your portfolio or resume a conversation without storing
  it.
- **Your own LLM API key** (if configured): stored encrypted at rest, never
  in plaintext, decrypted only in-memory at the moment a request is made on
  your behalf.
- **Telegram chat ID** (if you connect alerts): stored solely so the alert
  checker knows which chat to notify; not used for anything else.

## 8. What's not done with your data

This instance does not sell your data to third parties. Data is used to
provide the service to you, not for advertising or resale. (If you're an
operator running this for other people, this statement is only true if you
actually keep it true — don't ship this file unmodified if your instance's
practices differ.)
