# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ARIA to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background, identity or beliefs. We pledge to keep participation free of harassment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful to the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Promoting financial products or soliciting investments through the project's channels. ARIA is an education-only tool.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and ban people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on the repository or contact a maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
